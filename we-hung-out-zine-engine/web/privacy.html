Privacy

Back to the app · Last updated: November 2026

The short version: your photos stay on your device. We don't have accounts, we don't upload your photos, and we don't run analytics or ads.

What happens to your photos

  • When you add photos, your browser reads them, makes smaller copies, and keeps those copies in its own storage on your device so a refresh doesn't lose your work.
  • The zine PDF is built in your browser. Nothing is sent to us to make it.
  • To find your night, the app reads the date and time each photo was taken. It does not read your location. The smaller copies and the PDF don't carry the original photo's hidden metadata, such as location.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No uploading, selling or sharing your photos.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and no third-party fonts or scripts.

What is stored on your device

Your roll details (name, times), your page choices, and your resized photos, in the browser's local storage and IndexedDB. You can also choose a different blue for the page, which is stored the same way.

What our host can see

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like most websites, the host may keep standard technical logs, such as your IP address and which pages were requested. These logs don't contain your photos.

Deleting your data

  • Tap the yellow WHO button in the app and confirm to clear your current roll, photos and picks.
  • Or clear the site's data in your browser settings.

The PDF you download

The PDF is yours. If you share it or send it to a print shop, that's your choice, and it's handled under their rules.

If this changes

If we ever add accounts, cloud storage, AI analysis or payments, we'll update this page first and tell you clearly before any photo leaves your device.
